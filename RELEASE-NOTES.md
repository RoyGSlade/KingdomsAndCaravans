# Kingdoms & Caravans 0.3.0 — The Growing City

An early, unfinished, unsigned Windows x64 playtest. This release expands the city economy and incorporates the latest construction and troop-control playtest fixes.

## Build, supply and defend

- Guided opening, expanded map, finite trees, stone and iron deposits.
- Four food groups, diet-based happiness, restaurants, equipment workshops, armored recruitment and stone housing expansion.
- Two invasions at 6:00 and 14:00 after guided setup; Continue Building after victory.
- Archers climb stairs directly to a garrison or through completed, edge-connected walls and gate decks. They can fire from walls and garrisons. Broken connections block access until rebuilt.
- Green destination circles show troop orders and reserved elevated posts.
- Hover, click or press 1–9/0 to inspect a building and enter placement. Tab cycles categories; Defenses has its own category.
- Space or Freeze/Play stops simulation while leaving inspection and camera controls available. One construction is allowed per freeze, including the wall-section limit; save/load preserves the allowance.
- Defenses and selected workshops require stone alongside planks; blacksmiths and armor smiths also require iron. Costs are shown before placement. Stone/iron are committed on order while planks are hauled.
- Gatherers search the nearest reachable nondepleted resources without a maximum range. Distance still costs walking time.
- Stone roads are worker-built 2 × 2 sections costing 4 stone. Their 15% friendly movement bonus starts only after completion.

## Install and update

Extract the entire ZIP and run `KingdomsAndCaravans.exe`. The full guide is in `READ-ME.txt`; the package retains the original five-file layout. **Players on 0.2.1 must download and extract 0.3.0 manually once:** the old updater has a 100 MB download limit. Version 0.3.0 supports larger future updates through its menu. Keep the old folder for rollback.

Version-2 saves from 0.2.x remain supported. Legacy saves keep their original map dimensions and existing roads; start a new kingdom for the full map and guided opening. Version 0.1 saves require the old build.

## Verification and limits

The owner played the candidate and approved it. Local focused checks passed: 45 simulation checks and 29 native keyboard, mouse and layout checks. The paid economy playthrough completed 35 planned structures, both invasions, equipment production, armored recruitment, housing expansion and save restoration without injected supplies. Existing gameplay, UI and updater regressions were also exercised.

This remains a development-laptop playtest, not broad hardware qualification. Material costs are a first balance pass. Caravans, multiplayer, campaigns and research are not included. Please report your version, reproduction steps and expected behavior using the repository's playtest issue form.
